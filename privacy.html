Privacy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Short version: no cookies, no ads, no tracking, no accounts. The game counts playtime with a random number, not with your name, and you can turn that off.

Who is responsible

Jayraid is made by Tixx (Paco Interactive) in Switzerland. For anything about your data, write to jayraid.net@gmail.com.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub sees your IP address when you open a page and keeps it in its logs for security. I don't get those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The site saves nothing in your browser.

Claiming a tester place

When you claim a place, the site asks a small server at Cloudflare for a code. It stores your place number, the time, and a scrambled fingerprint of your IP address, so that clicking twice gives you the same code instead of a second place. The IP address itself is not stored and can't be read back from the fingerprint.

The launcher and the game

The launcher downloads the game and its updates from GitHub.

While you play, the game tells the same Cloudflare server every five minutes how long you played and which version you have. It sends a random number that the game picks on its first start, so I can count players without knowing who they are. No IP address, no name, nothing about your PC is stored. You can turn this off in the game under Options, Game, "Anonymous statistics".

Discord and itch.io

The Discord and the browser preview on itch.io are separate services with their own privacy rules. Nothing is sent to them unless you click the link.

Your rights

Under Swiss data protection law (and the GDPR if you live in the EU) you can ask what is stored about you and have it deleted. Since nothing above is tied to your name, I may need your tester number to find it. Write to jayraid.net@gmail.com.
