Jayraid is a sandbox first. Every world comes from a seed, is built from hexagon pillars and doesn't end. If you want more than building, there is a story with two bosses and an actual ending.

Blood moon

Some nights the moon turns red. Then the divers come for your base, and they bring field cannons. Whatever you built during the day had better hold.

Three ways to play

Sandbox if you just want to build. Adventure if you want the story. Challenge if twelve lives sound like too many: you get four.

Traders and divers

The traders sail between the islands and trade with you. The divers hold the camps and watchtowers. Their ships and towers shoot at each other, with or without you.

The bell

Every trader ship carries one, and it guards the golden wand. Hit it and it rings. The sound hurts. Take the wand and the traders won't forgive you.

Down in the caves

Green fires burn deep in the mountains, guarded by rotten divers. Now and then one gets back up blue and follows you. Give it a sabre.

The golem

Chained in an old castle ruin. Walk quietly and it sleeps. The sabre knocks it down, only the golden wand finishes it. Behind it waits the ending.
